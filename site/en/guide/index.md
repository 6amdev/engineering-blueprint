## What this system does
- Shows which meeting rooms are free at a given date and time
- Books a room ahead, with a subject and the number of attendees
- Lets you view, move or cancel your own bookings
- Notifies you when a booking succeeds and before the meeting starts

## Who can use it
| User | Can |
|---|---|
| Staff | See free rooms · book · change / cancel own bookings |
| Room admin | Everything staff can + add / close rooms · cancel others' bookings with a reason |

## What you need
- An organisation user account
- A current web browser on a phone or a computer

## Where to start
1. Sign in with your organisation account
2. Pick a date and time on the home page → choose a free room → confirm
